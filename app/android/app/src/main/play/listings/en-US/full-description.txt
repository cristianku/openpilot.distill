Distill is the companion app of distill-web, the control site of openpilot.distill: an open-source project that trains smaller, faster driving models for comma devices, learning from the big openpilot model and from your own drives.

The app works with your own Distill server, at the address you enter, and lets you follow and drive the work from the phone:

• Overview: GPU, CPU, memory and disk of the server, the jobs running with their progress, the training in progress and the state of your comma. Stop a job with one tap.
• Train: start a new model, continue from the stable model or resume a run that stopped; choose how long to train and see how much time it takes on your server.
• Evaluate: test a model on the drives it never saw and compare it with the model of your comma.
• Comma: install a finished model on your comma, after a checklist (reachable, parked, models menu), or put the original model back.
• Website: the whole distill-web site, signed in with the same session.

Every action goes through the API of your server, which checks it exactly as it checks the site: a button is enabled only when the server allows it, and says why not otherwise.

What you need: a Distill server (the free installer is at github.com/cristianku/openpilot.distill) that the phone can reach: on your own network, through a VPN or behind HTTPS. You sign in with the administrator of your site; the app keeps only the session, never the password.

No server yet? Type "demo" as the server address to try the app with made-up data.

Privacy: the app talks only to the server you enter. No account with us, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

Distill is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by comma.ai.
